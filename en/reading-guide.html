Three ways in

An investigation that crosses centuries

Two timelines answering each other: an organ-builder’s workshop in Braga in the second half of the eighteenth century, and the restoration of that same instrument in 2025. What is a decision in one is evidence in the other.

The past has your surname too

Inês is not investigating a stranger. She is investigating the possibility that her own family stood on the wrong side of the story — a question any reader can put to their own name.

Beauty can make horror worse

This is not a book about blood. It is about a sound everyone found sublime for two hundred and fifty years. That is where the Gothic settles: not in the dark, but in the admiration.

Do you need to know about organs?

No. The novel explains what it needs as it goes, the way you would show a visitor around a workshop. Readers who know organs will find more rigour than they expect; readers who know nothing are never left outside.

The atmosphere

Cold stone, a workshop floor of sawdust, archives, rain in the Minho, wax and molten metal. The pace is an investigation’s: patient at first, and then impossible to put down. Readers of Eco or Süskind will recognise the family — though this book talks less than Eco and measures more than Süskind.

Seven questions for a book club

For after reading. They reveal no more than the back cover, but they pay better once the book is finished.

  1. The book separates the one who orders a killing from the one who kills. Does that change the guilt, or only share it out?
  2. A masterpiece made out of a crime: do you destroy it, hide it, or show it with the label beside it?
  3. Madalena writes eight letters and delivers none. Is her silence weakness, strategy, or protection?
  4. Inês inherits a problem she did not create. What do we owe to an ancestor’s crime?
  5. Restoration means deciding what is preserved. If returning the instrument to its state erases the evidence, which should win?
  6. Tomás continues a craft whose method he did not choose. Is continuity complicity?
  7. If the sound is beautiful, does its origin matter? And to whom — the listener, the museum, the court?

Try the opening before deciding

The first pages are available in every store, in any of the five languages. If the Braga workshop holds you for ten pages, the rest is no longer in doubt.
